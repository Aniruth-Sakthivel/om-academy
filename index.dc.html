Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you enrol. Still unsure? Ask us directly

Q1 Who can take admission at OM Academy?
Students of any qualification level — from below 10th to postgraduates — can enrol. Each course has its own minimum eligibility, which our counsellors confirm with you at admission.
Q2 Are the certifications recognized outside Haryana?
Yes. NIELIT, HKCL and UGC-entitled degrees are recognized nationally, while HARTRON's HS-CIT programs are recognized by the Haryana Government for jobs and further education within the state.
Q3 How long do the courses take to complete?
It varies by program — short vocational and skill certificates run 4–12 weeks, IT certifications like O Level and CCC+ run 3–6 months, and UGC-entitled online degrees follow standard semester schedules.
Q4 Is scholarship or fee assistance available?
Yes — eligible students can apply for government scholarship schemes such as SAFAL, which offers up to 75% off course fees. Check our Announcements section for current openings and deadlines.
Q5 Do you help with placements after the course?
Yes. Every course includes placement assistance — interview preparation, resume support and introductions to our network of hiring partners — as part of the Get Placed stage of our career process.
